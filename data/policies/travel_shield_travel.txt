TRAVELSHIELD INTERNATIONAL TRAVEL INSURANCE
Policy Number: TS-2026-90511
Effective Date: February 15, 2026
Document Type: Fictional Travel Policy

1. GENERAL PROVISIONS & DEFINITIONS

1.1 Policy Coverage Period
Coverage begins on the scheduled departure date specified in the Certificate of Insurance and terminates upon completion of the insured trip or return to the country of residence, up to a maximum duration of 90 consecutive days per trip.

1.2 Definitions
- "Insured Traveler": The individual named in the insurance certificate for whom the premium has been paid prior to departure.
- "Emergency Medical Expenses": Medically necessary costs for doctor consultations, hospitalization, surgery, and prescribed medicines caused by sudden illness or injury during overseas travel.
- "Pre-Existing Medical Condition": Any medical condition, sickness, or injury for which medical treatment, diagnosis, or medication was received within 12 months prior to trip booking.

2. COVERED BENEFITS & LIMITS

2.1 Overseas Emergency Medical Expenses
TravelShield indemnifies reasonable and customary emergency medical and hospital expenses incurred outside the home country up to a maximum limit of $50,000 per traveler.

2.2 Emergency Medical Evacuation & Repatriation
In severe medical emergencies, TravelShield covers medically supervised transport to the nearest qualified hospital or repatriation to home country up to $25,000.

2.3 Trip Cancellation & Interruption
Reimburses non-refundable prepaid travel and accommodation expenses up to $10,000 if the trip is cancelled or interrupted due to sudden severe illness or death of the traveler or immediate family member.

2.4 Baggage Delay & Loss
(a) Baggage Delay: Pays up to $300 for essential clothing and toiletries if checked baggage is delayed by the airline for more than 12 consecutive hours.
(b) Total Loss of Baggage: Covers total loss of checked luggage by a common carrier up to $1,500.

2.5 Trip Flight Delay Allowance
Provides a cash allowance of $50 for every completed 6 hours of flight delay beyond the scheduled departure time, up to a maximum payout of $500.

3. EXCLUSIONS & WAITING PERIODS

3.1 General Exclusions
TravelShield does not cover any loss, expense, or claim arising from:
(a) Pre-existing medical conditions, unless optional Pre-Existing Condition Coverage Endorsement was purchased prior to travel;
(b) Participation in extreme sports, mountaineering, skydiving, scuba diving below 30 meters, or professional athletic competitions;
(c) Travel undertaken against the advice of a certified medical practitioner;
(d) Suicide, intentional self-harm, or exposure to hazardous occupational conditions;
(e) Volcanic eruptions, nuclear hazards, war, acts of terrorism, or government border closures.

3.2 Medical Waiting Period
A mandatory 48-hour waiting period applies from the time of crossing the home country border before non-accidental sickness benefits become eligible under Section 2.1. Emergency accidental injury coverage has no waiting period.

4. DEDUCTIBLES & APPLICABLE EXCESS

4.1 Medical Expenses Deductible
A compulsory deductible of $100 applies to each emergency medical claim submitted under Section 2.1.

4.2 Baggage Claim Deductible
A compulsory deductible of $50 applies to total luggage loss claims under Section 2.4(b).

5. CLAIM SUBMISSION & NOTIFICATION DUTIES

5.1 Immediate 24-Hour Notice Requirement
The traveler or representative must contact TravelShield 24/7 Assistance Helpline within 24 hours of hospitalization, emergency evacuation, or trip cancellation.

5.2 Required Supporting Evidence
All claims must be supported by original documentation submitted within 30 days of returning home:
(a) Official Claim Form detailing the incident;
(b) Passport copy showing entry/exit immigration stamps;
(c) Original medical receipts, hospital discharge summary, and doctor reports;
(d) Airline confirmation of baggage delay or Property Irregularity Report (PIR);
(e) Proof of prepaid non-refundable trip receipts.

6. GENERAL CONDITIONS & CANCELLATION

6.1 Policy Cancellation
The insured may cancel this policy prior to the start date for a full refund minus a $15 service fee. No refund is granted once travel has commenced.
