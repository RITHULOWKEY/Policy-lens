1. GENERAL INFORMATION & DEFINITIONS

1.1 Policy Scope & Eligibility
This RoadGuard Motor Insurance Policy is effective from March 1, 2026. Coverage extends to private passenger automobiles listed in the policy declaration, operated by licensed drivers aged 18 to 75.

1.2 Key Definitions
- "Insured Vehicle": The registered private vehicle described in the schedule, including standard factory fittings and installed safety equipment.
- "Accidental Loss": Sudden, unexpected physical damage caused by external violent and visible means.
- "Deductible Amount": The fixed initial portion of any claim payable by the insured prior to coverage payout.

2. COVERAGE & EXTENSIONS

2.1 Comprehensive & Collision Damage
RoadGuard covers physical loss or damage caused by collision, overturning, fire, theft, attempted theft, explosion, lightning, storm, tempest, and malicious acts up to the insured declared value ($60,000 max limit).

2.2 Third-Party Liability Coverage
Legal liability for third-party property damage is covered up to $30,000 per accident. Third-party bodily injury or death liability is indemnified up to $75,000 per person and $150,000 per event.

2.3 Roadside Assistance & Breakdown Service
Includes 24/7 emergency towing up to 75 kilometers, roadside flat tire replacement, emergency fuel top-up, and lock-out service assistance.

2.4 Rental Vehicle Allowance Extension
In the event of an authorized collision repair taking longer than 48 hours, RoadGuard provides a temporary rental vehicle allowance of up to $40 per day for a maximum of 10 consecutive days.

3. EXCLUSIONS & LIMITATIONS

3.1 Statutory Exclusions
No liability is accepted for claims where:
(a) The driver is intoxicated or under the influence of illicit drugs or unauthorized medication;
(b) The vehicle is driven without a valid driver's license or under license suspension;
(c) The vehicle is used for commercial delivery, taxi services, car pooling for fee, or competitive racing;
(d) Loss is intentionally caused or staged by the insured.

3.2 Specific Perils Excluded
This policy excludes:
(a) Wear and tear, mechanical breakdown, or tire punctures unsupported by collision damage;
(b) Earthquakes, volcanic activity, flood, landslide, or inundation unless optional Natural Calamity Rider is attached;
(c) Damage caused by riots, strikes, civil commotion, war, or radioactive contamination.

4. DEDUCTIBLES & FINANCIAL LIMITS

4.1 Collision Claim Deductible
A compulsory deductible of $750 applies to every collision claim filed under Section 2.1.

4.2 Comprehensive Non-Collision Deductible
A compulsory deductible of $350 applies to non-collision damage, including theft and fire claims.

4.3 Excess Waiver Rider
If the insured repairs the vehicle at a RoadGuard Priority Network Workshop, the applicable deductible is reduced by 50%.

5. CLAIMS & NOTIFICATION DUTIES

5.1 Notice of Loss Timeline
The insured must report any accident, theft, or damage to RoadGuard claims helpline or mobile app within 72 hours of occurrence.

5.2 Claim Documents Required
The following documents must be submitted within 14 days of filing:
(a) Completed Claim Form signed by the driver;
(b) Police First Information Report (FIR) for theft, third-party injuries, or major accidents exceeding $1,000 damage;
(c) Valid Driver's License and Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC);
(d) Repair estimate from an authorized garage;
(e) Photos of damaged vehicle parts.

6. CANCELLATION & RECOVERY

6.1 Insured Initiated Cancellation
The insured may cancel this policy by giving 15 days written notice. A short-period refund scale applies provided no claims were registered during the policy period.

6.2 Insurer Initiated Cancellation
RoadGuard reserves the right to cancel this policy on 10 days notice due to fraud, non-payment, or willful misrepresentation.
