1. GENERAL INFORMATION & DEFINITIONS

1.1 Policy Effective Date & Scope
This SecureDrive Motor Insurance Policy takes effect on January 1, 2026, at 00:01 AM standard local time. Coverage applies to the registered vehicle specified in the policy schedule while operated within designated legal territories by authorized drivers aged 21 to 70 holding a valid driver's license.

1.2 Definitions
- "Policyholder": The individual or entity named in the policy schedule holding legal title to the insured vehicle.
- "Covered Vehicle": The motor vehicle described in the policy declaration schedule, including factory-installed original equipment.
- "Collision": An accidental impact between the covered vehicle and another vehicle, object, or person, or rollover of the vehicle.
- "Constructive Total Loss": A condition where the estimated repair cost of the covered vehicle exceeds 75% of its Actual Cash Value immediately preceding the accident.
- "Deductible": The specific amount that the policyholder must pay out-of-pocket towards covered loss before insurer indemnification begins.

2. COVERAGE & BENEFITS

2.1 Accidental Collision Coverage
The insurer will pay for direct physical loss or damage to the covered vehicle resulting from accidental collision or rollover. Payout covers authorized repair costs or Actual Cash Value up to the Maximum Benefit limit, less the collision deductible.

2.2 Comprehensive Non-Collision Coverage
Coverage is extended for direct physical loss or damage caused by fire, lightning, explosion, theft, attempted theft, vandalism, falling objects, windstorm, hail, or collision with animals.

2.3 Third-Party Property Damage & Bodily Injury Liability
The insurer will indemnify the policyholder for legal liability arising from an accident causing third-party property damage (up to $25,000) or third-party bodily injury/death (up to $50,000 per person, $100,000 per accident).

2.4 Emergency Roadside & Towing Assistance
In the event of vehicle breakdown or collision disabling the covered vehicle, roadside assistance provides towing up to 50 kilometers to the nearest repair center, battery jump-start, emergency fuel delivery, and flat tire replacement.

3. EXCLUSIONS & LIMITATIONS

3.1 General Exclusions
No coverage or benefit shall be provided under any section of this policy for loss or damage occurring while:
(a) The covered vehicle is operated by any person under the influence of alcohol, narcotics, or non-prescribed controlled substances;
(b) The driver does not possess a valid, active driver's license;
(c) The vehicle is engaged in racing, speed testing, rally events, or commercial ride-hailing/delivery services unless specifically endorsed;
(d) Loss results from intentional acts, gross negligence, or fraudulent activity by the policyholder or authorized driver.

3.2 Uncovered Damage & Perils
This policy strictly excludes:
(a) Mechanical breakdown, electrical failure, or wear and tear;
(b) Damage caused by earthquakes, volcanic eruptions, tsunamis, floods, or natural landslides;
(c) Loss resulting from war, invasion, civil unrest, terrorism, or nuclear contamination;
(d) Damage to aftermarket non-factory sound systems, custom paint, or performance modifications unless declared and endorsed.

3.3 Geographical Restrictions
Coverage is limited exclusively to roads within the domestic country of issuance. Driving across international land borders immediately suspends all coverage under this policy unless an International Transit Endorsement has been issued prior to departure.

4. LIMITS AND DEDICATED DEDUCTIBLES

4.1 Maximum Benefit Limits
The total maximum liability of the insurer for physical vehicle damage shall not exceed $50,000 per collision incident, subject to an aggregate policy annual limit of $100,000 for all claims submitted during the 12-month policy term.

4.2 Collision Deductible
A mandatory out-of-pocket deductible of $500 applies to each and every collision claim submitted under Section 2.1. The deductible is subtracted directly from the total approved repair invoice or loss settlement.

4.3 Comprehensive Deductible
A mandatory out-of-pocket deductible of $250 applies to each comprehensive non-collision claim under Section 2.2, except for glass-only repair claims where the deductible is waived.

5. CLAIMS AND OBLIGATIONS

5.1 Claim Notification Period
The policyholder must notify the insurer in writing or via the customer portal within 48 hours of any accident, collision, theft, or vandalism incident. Late notification exceeding 7 business days without reasonable cause may result in denial of the claim.

5.2 Required Claim Documentation
To process any claim, the policyholder must submit:
(a) Fully completed and signed Official Claim Form;
(b) Official Police Accident Report (mandatory for collision and theft claims);
(c) Copy of driver's license and vehicle registration certificate;
(d) Clear photographic evidence of vehicle damage and scene of accident;
(e) Written itemized repair estimate from an insurer-authorized repair center.

5.3 Dispute & Settlement Resolution
The insurer retains the absolute right to inspect the covered vehicle prior to repair authorization and to direct repairs to designated certified network repair facilities.

6. POLICY TERMS & CANCELLATION

6.1 Cancellation by Policyholder
The policyholder may cancel this policy at any time by providing 30 days prior written notice. Upon cancellation, the insurer will refund unearned pro-rata premium minus a $50 administrative fee, provided no claims have been filed during the current policy term.

6.2 Cancellation by Insurer
The insurer may cancel this policy upon 14 days written notice sent to the policyholder's registered address in cases of non-payment of premium, material misrepresentation, or fraud.
